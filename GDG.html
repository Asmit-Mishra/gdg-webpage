What is GDG ?

Google Developer Groups (GDG) is a global community for anyone passionate about technology and open-source. It's a platform to learn, create, and collaborate across diverse domains. Through engaging workshops, exciting hackathons, and insightful expert talks, GDG empowers members to transform curiosity into impactful projects while building meaningful connections, exchanging knowledge, and expanding career opportunities within the vibrant tech ecosystem.
